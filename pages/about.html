About StudentHub

StudentHub is a modern digital campus portal designed to make student life easier. It provides a single platform where students can register, log in, participate in events, check notices, manage their profiles, and stay connected with campus activities.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide students with a smart, simple, and secure platform for managing academic and campus activities.

🚀 Our Vision

To create a fully digital campus where students can access every important service anytime and anywhere.

⭐ Key Features

  • Student Registration
  • Secure Login
  • Profile Management
  • Event Registration
  • Latest Notices
  • Campus Dashboard
