Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about StudentHub.

❓ How do I register on StudentHub?

Click on the Register page, fill in your details, and submit the registration form.

❓ How do I log in?

Open the Login page, enter your email or enrollment number and password, then click Login.

❓ Can I update my profile?

Yes. Visit the Profile page and click the Edit Profile button.

❓ How can I register for an event?

Open the Event page and click the Register button for the event you want to join.

❓ Where can I see the latest notices?

The latest notices are available on the Home page and Dashboard.

❓ Who should I contact if I face any problem?

Visit the Contact page and send us a message using the contact form.
